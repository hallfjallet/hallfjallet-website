Press

Hållfjället is a restaurant with rooms in a 1943 mountain station above Ottsjö in the southern Åre mountains. There is no road. The trail runs for nearly eight kilometres, winding up from Ottsjö through open fell, and dinner is cooked for the 24 guests who have made the climb.

Hållfjället is run by Linda and Rodrigo Pérez, who come from restaurants rather than from the mountains or the hotel trade, and who have kept a restaurant’s ambitions in a place where the weather decides when the deliveries come.

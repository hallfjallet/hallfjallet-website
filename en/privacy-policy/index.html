Privacy policy

We take good care of your information. Here we briefly explain which personal data we receive through the website and how we look after it.

Who is responsible

Hållfjällsgården AB is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at info@hallfjallet.se or +46 647 690 466.

The newsletter

When you sign up for our newsletter, we keep your first name, last name and email address. We use them only to send you the newsletter, and we do so with your consent.

The information is stored with our provider Mailchimp, which may store it outside the EU. Any transfer is made with the safeguards required by EU rules.

Every newsletter has a link to unsubscribe. When you do, we stop sending, and we do not keep your information for longer than you are subscribed.

When you book

Bookings are made through Bookvisit. We use the information you give when booking to welcome you and arrange your stay. Bookvisit has its own privacy policy, which you can find on the booking page.

When you write to us

If you write to us, we use your information to reply and to help you with your stay.

Cookies

The website does not use cookies for statistics or marketing.

Your rights

You have the right to know what information we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. Get in touch and we will help you. If you are not happy with how we handle your information, you can contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Activities in the Mountains

There are many ways to get to know the mountains. On skis through snow-laden forests, with a fishing rod over a frozen lake, or in the company of someone who knows the land, the old trails and those little places you won’t quite find on a map. We have gathered a few of the days we love most ourselves.

Together with our local guides, we venture out into the mountains and forests, sometimes in search of a little adventure, sometimes with no particular purpose other than to enjoy ourselves. A fire is lit, the coffee pot comes out, and before long, something warm and delicious is ready to eat.

Winter

Skiing to Hållfjället

We can’t think of a better way to reach Hållfjället than on skis. Through old mountain forests, across frozen marshes and along winding trails that eventually lead us up to the lodge.

Together with our local guides, we set off on a day where the journey matters every bit as much as the destination. There are stories to be told, little detours to take and beautiful places worth stopping for. We’ll light a fire, put the coffee on and take our time.

We usually set off from Ottsjö, but for those who fancy a longer day on skis, it may also be possible to begin in Edsåsdalen or Trillevallen. The route is planned together with our guides, taking the weather, snow conditions and everyone’s skiing experience into account.

By the time we reach Hållfjället, the wood-fired sauna is warm, something good is cooking and a long dinner awaits. Perhaps a cold beer or a particularly fine bottle from the cellar, too.

Ice fishing in the mountains

Out on the ice of a little mountain lake, with the snow all around us and a small hole leading down into the dark water, we wait for the day’s catch. Ice fishing calls for a certain amount of patience, but fortunately, very little hurry.

Together with our local guides, we make our way to one of the little lakes tucked away in the mountains. Depending on the weather and snow conditions, we travel on skis, snowshoes or by snowmobile. Our guides know these waters, have a good idea of where the fish might be hiding and usually have a story or two to tell while we wait for a bite.

Before long, we light a fire, put the coffee on and prepare something rather good to eat. Perhaps it will be the day’s catch, or perhaps something we wisely brought along with us. Either way, it tends to be a very fine day indeed.

We are happy to arrange the outing around your wishes and the conditions in the mountains.

Through winter by dog sled

First come the eager barks of the dogs, their paws dancing impatiently in the snow. Then we’re off. The sled glides between the trees, the dogs settle into their rhythm, and soon there is little to hear but the gentle whisper of the runners against the snow and the sound of the dogs breathing.

In the company of experienced mushers, we travel through winter forests and across open stretches of snowy countryside. It is a wonderful way to get to know the dogs, learn a little about their work and see the landscape from an entirely different perspective. Here, the dogs lead the way, with a rather infectious enthusiasm for the task at hand.

The outing is planned around the weather, snow conditions and the wishes of the group. Perhaps we’ll stop for a cup of coffee and something good to eat, while the dogs enjoy a well-earned rest.

We are happy to arrange a dog sledding experience as part of your stay at Hållfjället.

Snowmobile transfers

Not everyone needs to make their way to Hållfjället under their own steam. For those who would rather give their skis a rest, we arrange snowmobile transfers from Ottsjö, Edsåsdalen or Åre, through the winter landscape and up towards the mountain lodge.

The journey takes us through snow-laden forests and across open stretches of mountain terrain before Hållfjället eventually comes into view. We collect you from an agreed meeting point and make sure that both you and your luggage arrive safely.

Transfers can be arranged one way or as a return journey, and we are always happy to help plan your arrival so that the journey up is as straightforward as possible.

By the time you reach Hållfjället, the wood-fired sauna is warm and the house is ready to welcome you. Not a bad way to begin a stay in the mountains.

Summer

Hunting beyond the roads

An early morning, long before the mountains have properly woken. The dogs are eager, the equipment is packed and ahead lies a day in country where few people ever venture.

Together with experienced hunting guides, we arrange hunting expeditions on private grounds in the mountains of Jämtland. By helicopter, we travel out to remote hunting grounds, where the dogs, the weather and the terrain set the pace of the day. Patience, good judgement and a willingness to let things take their time are all rather useful qualities out here.

We take care of the arrangements, tailoring each hunting trip to the wishes and experience of the group, with local guides, transport and good food all part of the experience.

At the end of the day, we return to Hållfjället. Perhaps the hunt has been successful, or perhaps the mountains had other plans. Either way, there is usually a story or two to tell around the dinner table.

Stories from the mountain

The gardens are resting, the cellar is waiting and winter is on it

There is a particular kind of stillness in the mountains once the season comes to an end.

The last guests have made their way down the trail and the mountains are slowly changing color. After a beautiful season filled with long dinners, wonderful wines and familiar faces returning to visit us, we find ourselves feeling immensely grateful.

A season gathered and preserved

It has been a relatively cold summer here in Jämtland, and the gardens have taken their time. But nature has its own rhythm, and we’ve been happy to follow it.

For us, this is one of the most beautiful parts of working with the seasons. Nothing quite disappears. It simply takes another form.
